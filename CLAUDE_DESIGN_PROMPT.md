# Prompt for Claude Design

Create a high-fidelity visual design for the Russian personal professional homepage of Tatiana Miroshina.

Do NOT copy the visual design of criteriodigitale.it. Treat it only as context for the professional subject. The Russian identity must be independent and more personal.

## Positioning

Tatiana is not a generic AI consultant, coach, agency, or software expert.

She works with complexity. First she understands what is actually happening, identifies context, relationships, constraints and decision criteria, and only then chooses a structure, process, digital tool or AI.

Key visitor feeling:

“Here I can bring a complicated, messy or badly-formulated problem. Nobody will sell me a tool before understanding the situation.”

## Concept

LIVING SYSTEM

INTELLECTUAL EDITORIAL × SYSTEMS CARTOGRAPHY × ITALIAN MATERIALITY

The page should feel like a sophisticated intellectual magazine, a systems map and a tactile European object at the same time.

The visual idea is:
FIELD → RELATIONS → STRUCTURE → POSSIBLE ACTION

Do NOT turn this into CHAOS → ORDER.

## Person / background

Tatiana has 20+ years of practical entrepreneurship. She built her own product brand, worked with artisan manufacturing, designed products and selected materials, managed international B2B/B2C sales, multiple multilingual online shops, SEO and marketing, CITES-regulated processes, and later real-estate/B&B operations and educational-platform technical work.

Her intellectual fields include systems thinking, complexity, emergence, AI, digital processes, philosophy, ethics, existential analysis, decision-making, autonomy and continuous education.

AI is not the ideology of the brand. It is one possible instrument inside a larger system.

## Visual system

Colors:
Warm Ivory #F3EFE7
Paper #FBFAF7
Graphite #1D2224
Deep Petrol #153D3A
Oxidized Copper #A9644C
Grey Sage #7C8982
Lines #D8D2C7

Typography:
Use Literata or comparable Cyrillic editorial serif for major thought-led headings.
Use Manrope or comparable Cyrillic sans-serif for UI, navigation and system annotations.

Serif = thought / human / meaning.
Sans = system / data / navigation / tool.

Use generous whitespace, asymmetric editorial layouts, thin rules, restrained rectangular buttons, very few cards.

## Signature graphic language

Use a refined MAP OF RELATIONS:
nodes, thin lines, branches, incomplete connections, annotations, coordinates and open fields.

Possible concepts inside the map:
контекст
человек
ресурсы
ограничения
система
AI
решение
действие

Avoid IT-network aesthetics. Make it feel editorial and intelligent.

## Photography

Natural daylight, European, editorial, tactile. Stone, wood, paper, architecture, real working environment.

Do not use generic businesswoman-with-laptop, coaching, glamour or mystical imagery.

Do not use literal runes, tarot or occult symbolism.

## Page structure

1. HERO
Small label: Системы / AI / человек

Headline:
Сначала увидеть, что действительно происходит.

Second line:
Потом решать, что с этим делать.

Lead:
Помогаю экспертам и небольшим проектам разобраться в сложной задаче, увидеть возможности и собрать работающую систему — от продукта и процессов до цифровых инструментов и AI.

CTA:
Принести задачу →

Secondary:
Как я работаю

Use a relationship-map visual in the hero.

2. ЧТО КО МНЕ ОБЫЧНО ПРИНОСЯТ
Do not call it “Услуги”.
Show several different entry states into one system rather than SaaS cards.

3. Я НЕ НАЧИНАЮ С ИНСТРУМЕНТА
Visualize:
контекст → отношения → варианты → критерии → структура → инструменты → действие

4. ЧТО ПОЯВЛЯЕТСЯ НА ВЫХОДЕ
Possible forms: map, criteria, product structure, process architecture, workflow, site/landing, documentation, automation, AI-assisted workflow, next grounded action.

5. HUMAN LAYER
Headline:
В любой системе остаётся человек.

Make this visually quieter, almost like an essay page.

6. ПОЧЕМУ Я ТАК РАБОТАЮ
Show a trajectory, not a CV:
PRODUCT → CRAFT / PRODUCTION → INTERNATIONAL E-COMMERCE → DIGITAL → PROCESSES → SYSTEMS → AI → HUMAN LAYER

7. OBSERVATORY / THINKING
Future space for research, articles, AI workshops, experiments, cases and author products.

8. FINAL CTA
Не уверены, как называется ваша задача?
Это нормальное место для начала.

CTA:
Принести задачу →

## Motion

Very restrained. A few lines can reveal slowly. Relationships may appear on hover or scroll. No cursor gimmicks or constant animation. Respect reduced motion.

## Explicit prohibitions

No purple/blue AI gradients.
No neon.
No robots.
No AI brains.
No sparkles.
No glassmorphism.
No glowing blobs.
No generic SaaS card grids.
No large pill buttons.
No mystical imagery.
No aggressive sales aesthetic.

## Deliverable

First create the coherent visual system, then produce a high-fidelity 1440px desktop homepage and key 390px mobile views. The design should be strong enough to become the base language for future product pages, research, blog, presentations and social materials.
